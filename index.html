From landing to reunion

What happens on arrival

The part owners worry about most, step by step.

Arrival & transfer

Your pet is unloaded and moved to the animal holding area, with food and water while arrival checks run.

Documents review

Health records and paperwork are checked so everything is in order for entry.

Veterinary inspection

Where required, an authorised vet inspects your pet before clearance.

Customs & clearance

Final checks are completed before your pet is cleared for release.

Release & handover

Your pet goes to you, or to quarantine if the destination requires it. Ready for the next chapter.

Rules differ by destination and change over time. CAS confirms the exact requirements for your pet and route before anything is booked.

Small details matter

Help your pet feel at home on the way

🧺

Get comfy with the crate

Spend relaxed time in the travel crate and introduce it in a calm, positive way well before departure.

👕

Send a familiar scent

A familiar blanket or a thin T-shirt with your smell gives your pet something recognisable in new places.

🕰️

Keep routines steady

Keep feeding and walks as consistent as possible, and avoid sudden changes just before the flight.
